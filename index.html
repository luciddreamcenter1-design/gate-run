How it works

Filmmakers make one film.
This makes one for every player.

01 · SELFIE

You become a Crasher

FLUX 3 on Comfy Router redraws your selfie as a semi-realistic manhwa hero who still looks like you.

02 · TRAILER

Your own anime trailer

LTX-2 image-to-video with a camera LoRA animates three shots of you on Comfy Cloud. A narrator says your name. The browser cuts it together with title cards and grade.

03 · FIGHT

Then you play as you

LTX-2 animates your full body (idle, run, a 3-hit katana combo, dash, special). The browser keys and bakes it into sprites. You fight as yourself.

The game

Endless Gates. Real combat.

A boss every fifth Gate

Each Gate is harder than the last. Manual combos with aim assist, dash i-frames, a special meter, hit-stop and juggles. Every fifth Gate a Breach Lord walks out of the rift. Weapons drop between Gates: Flame Katana, Thunder Blade, Rift Twin Daggers, and the Hollow King's Cleaver.

1v1 a friend by link

Tap "1v1 a friend online" and send the link. You fight live, peer-to-peer in the browser with no game server. VS splash, voiced quotes, a K.O. cinematic, and a victory card that says "Think you can take me?"

Pick a Crasher, free forever

No selfie? Four original fighters ship with the game, each with a full LTX-animated moveset and a voiced line.

Fight anywhere, even your own street

Four living arenas, each a FLUX 3 painting animated by LTX-2. Or upload a photo of your place and ComfyUI turns it into a rift-torn battleground.

Under the hood

Two Comfy APIs, one SDK, LTX at the core.

A small Python server sends each job down a route list and uses the first route that works. If a provider is down or out of credits, it falls through to the next one.
